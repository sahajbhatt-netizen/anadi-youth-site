Privacy Policy

How Anadi Youth handles information when you browse the site, use optional forms, or open third-party services.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Anadi Youth is designed to be usable without creating an account. The current core site does not sell products, require sign-in, or intentionally use advertising trackers. This policy explains the limited information that may still be processed when the website is delivered or when you choose to interact with third-party services.

Information that may be processed

Technical information

When you request a page, the hosting and infrastructure used to deliver the site may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device type, requested URL, date and time, and related request or security data. GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

Information you choose to provide

If you choose to contact Anadi Youth, complete an external form, or voluntarily submit feedback, the information you provide may be processed so we can respond, operate the activity, or improve the site. A form or third-party service may provide its own privacy notice.

How information is used

  • to deliver, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the website;
  • to respond when you contact us or participate in an optional activity;
  • to understand and improve content or site functionality when feedback is provided; and
  • to meet legal, safety, or security obligations where applicable.

Anadi Youth does not sell personal information and does not currently use the core website for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.

Hosting, fonts, links, and embedded content

The site currently uses managed third-party infrastructure, including GitHub Pages for static hosting and Google-hosted web fonts. Your browser may connect directly to those providers to receive site resources.

The site also links to platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, Medium, Reddit, and Spotify. If you follow those links—or if a future page embeds third-party media—the third party may receive information and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies.

Cookies and analytics

The current Anadi Youth core site does not require an account and is not designed around advertising cookies. We do not currently operate our own behavioral-advertising system.

If we later add analytics, personalization, consent-managed cookies, or materially different tracking, we will review this policy and provide any notices or choices required for that use.

Young people and children

Anadi Youth is youth-facing, so we aim to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. The core site does not ask visitors to create profiles or accounts.

Children under 13 should not send personal information directly to Anadi Youth through optional forms or messages without the involvement of a parent or guardian. If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 was submitted directly to us in a way that should not have occurred, a parent or guardian may contact us to request review or deletion.

Retention and security

Information voluntarily provided to Anadi Youth is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was provided, subject to applicable organizational, legal, or security requirements. Hosting and third-party providers may maintain their own logs and retention schedules.

No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to limit data collection and use managed services appropriate for the site.

Your choices and requests

You can use the core public site without an Anadi Youth account. You can also choose not to open third-party links, embedded media, or optional forms.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights regarding personal information you have provided. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact Anadi Youth through its official channels in the footer or through the official SMVS contact information at smvs.org. We may need enough information to understand and verify the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the site, its providers, or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version.

These pages are written to reflect how the Anadi Youth website operates today. If the site later adds accounts, payments, targeted advertising, new analytics, or materially different data collection, these policies should be reviewed and updated.
