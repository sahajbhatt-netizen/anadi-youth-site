Resources

Sometimes you want to follow a journey from beginning to end.

Other times, you already know what you are looking for.

Resources brings together the places you can listen, read, revisit spiritual practices, explore prayers and kirtans, and go deeper through books and scriptures.

Watch & listen

Bring something meaningful into the time you already spend listening.

Explore Anadi Youth content on Spotify, or listen to Guruji's Katha-Varta and other SMVS content through the official SMVS YouTube channel. Much of the content on the SMVS YouTube channel is in Gujarati, while Anadi Youth's Spotify content provides another way to explore spirituality and personal growth in English.

Some ideas deserve more time than a short post or conversation can give them.

Our articles explore spirituality, philosophy, self-understanding, personal growth, Guruji's guidance, and what it can look like to bring these ideas into everyday life.

Prayers & kirtans

Sometimes you do not need another explanation.

You need words that help you remember Maharaj, express gratitude, surrender, pray, reflect, or reconnect with something you already know but have not felt deeply in a while.

Explore English and Gujarati prayers and kirtans with their lyrics, meanings, spiritual context, and guidance for how they can become part of your own practice.

Over time, this library will continue to grow.

Books & scriptures

Some questions need more room.

Books and scriptures give you the opportunity to stay with an idea longer, understand where our teachings come from, and explore Gnan beyond what can fit into a single article or Sabha.

A few English resources you can begin with:

Holy Vachanamrut

Explore Shriji Maharaj's teachings through an English edition of the Vachanamrut.

Essence of Shikshapatri

Explore the principles and guidance Shriji Maharaj gave for living a disciplined spiritual life.

Vani: The Art of Speech

Explore how the way we speak affects ourselves, our relationships, and the people around us.

These are only a starting point. SMVS maintains a broader collection of English and Gujarati books, scriptures, and other Satsang literature.

Explore the Full SMVS Books & Scriptures Library →

Use what you find.

A resource is useful when it leads somewhere.

A Katha may give you a question to carry into the week. An article may help you see yourself differently. A practice guide may help you return to something that had become mechanical. A prayer may give words to something you were struggling to express. A scripture may open a question that takes much longer to understand.

You do not need to consume everything.

Find what helps you take the next step, use it, reflect on what happens, and return when you are ready to go deeper.
