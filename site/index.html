Karan Satsang

A journey from knowledge to experience.

Karan Satsang asks us to understand ourselves, understand God, and gradually allow that understanding to change the way we live and experience life. That can involve asking deeper questions, examining ourselves honestly, developing spiritual practices, learning from the experienced ones, and growing alongside others. The goal is not simply to know more about spirituality. It is to become different through it.

About Anadi Youth

Make space for something deeper.
