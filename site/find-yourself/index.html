Find Yourself

You spend your whole life with yourself, but that does not necessarily mean you understand yourself well.

Most of us can describe our personality, interests, strengths, relationships, and the roles we play. The harder questions usually sit underneath those descriptions. Why do certain things matter so much to me? Why do I keep reacting the same way? How much of what I want is actually mine? What kind of person am I becoming?

Find Yourself is a place to explore those questions through your own life.

Our Gnan takes the question of identity much deeper. If you want to begin there first, explore Who Am I? in Gnan & Philosophy.

Start with four questions ↓Explore Who Am I? →

Look inward as you go.

The pages in this journey are not meant to be read once and checked off.

When a question catches your attention, connect it to something real. A decision you made. A conversation that bothered you. A habit you keep repeating. Something you wanted badly. A moment when another person's opinion changed what you did.

Then stay with it long enough to ask why.

That kind of honest inward reflection is what we call Nij-Darshan. You do not need to know the entire method before you begin. The journey itself will give you questions and small experiments to practice with.

Learn Nij-Darshan →

Start with these four questions.

01

What is actually yours?

We are shaped by family, friends, culture, school, work, social media, and the communities around us. Begin by noticing where those influences end and your own individuality begins.

→
02

Who are you becoming?

Your habits, decisions, values, strengths, weaknesses, and responsibilities are slowly taking you somewhere. Think about the direction you actually want your life to move in.

→
03

What Is Happening Inside You?

What happens around you is only part of your experience. Explore what happens within you: what you notice, feel, assume, carry forward, and the patterns that keep shaping how you experience life.

→
04

What is your role in the life around you?

Understanding yourself eventually has to affect how you live. Your choices matter to other people, and the roles you carry come with real responsibilities.

→
Keep returning

Keep using what you notice.

You may come through these questions once and find useful answers. Come back a year later and you may see something completely different.

That is normal. Your life keeps giving you new material.

Nij-Darshan gives you a way to keep working with it: notice what happened, look at what happened within you, understand the pattern if there is one, and decide what you want to do with what you learned.

NoticeWhat happened?
Look inwardWhat happened inside me?
Ask whyWhy?
Look for the patternHave I seen this before?
RespondWhat do I want to do with what I learned?

Sometimes you will be able to see something clearly on your own. Sometimes another perspective will help you notice what you are missing.
